About the Project

AgroWatch is a Final-Year B.Tech ICT project built to support smallholder farmers in Ghana. Many farmers grow tomato, maize, and pineapple, but checking every plant by walking the farm takes time, and pests or diseases can spread before help arrives. Selling produce is also hard when farmers do not know buyers or fair prices.

This system helps in a simple way: you take or upload a photo of your crop. The system finds the plants in the picture, checks for common pests and diseases, and tells you what to do in everyday language. You can also view past scans, list produce for sale, and see local markets — all on one website that works on a phone or computer.

What You Can Do

  • Find plants in photos — works for tomato, maize, and pineapple
  • Follow plants across photos — helpful when the camera moves
  • Get health advice — common pests and diseases, with simple next steps
  • Sell better — list produce and see local markets
  • Different users — views for farmers, buyers, experts, and admin
